Platform features

Everything the library runs on.

One system covers the shelf, the borrowing desk, overdue fees, and the reminders that bring books back on time.

Catalog

Titles, copies, and what is on the shelf

  • Book records

    Title, subject label, optional author, and an active state for every title.

  • Physical copies

    Each copy carries its own unique code, separate from the title.

  • Live availability

    Copies on loan, lost, or withdrawn are excluded automatically.

  • Search and scan

    Find titles by title, author, or subject; staff can search by copy code.

  • Copy status

    Mark a copy lost or withdrawn, guarded while it is on loan.

  • Paged lists

    Catalog and console tables show 50 rows at a time.

Borrowing

Requests, decisions, and due dates

  • Borrow requests

    A reason plus a 3-day, 5-day, or 1-week loan.

  • Copy selection

    Pick an available copy code or ask the librarian to supply one.

  • Due-date preview

    Borrow and due dates are calculated before the student submits.

  • Approve or decline

    Atomic decisions with a note; the student is notified either way.

  • Cancel a request

    Students can withdraw a request while it is still pending.

  • Desk lookup

    Search requests by School ID, title, author, or copy code.

Returns and fees

Closing loans and settling what is owed

  • Return by code

    Scan or type a copy code, or return straight from the borrowed row.

  • Loan status

    Not yet, Returned, and Overdue are shown to students and staff.

  • Overdue fees

    ₱10 per overdue day, frozen against the loan when it is returned.

  • Collect or waive

    Record payment or a waiver with the staff member, time, and note.

  • Haven't returned

    Every past-due active loan in one staff view.

  • Student balance

    Patrons see their outstanding fee total in the portal.

Reminders and alerts

Reaching students before the due date

  • Due-date reminders

    Generated for loans due tomorrow, due today, and overdue.

  • Four channels

    In-app inbox, email, SMS, and browser push, enabled independently.

  • Delivery log

    Every attempt keeps its status, recipient, provider id, or error.

  • Retries

    Failed sends retry with backoff before staff see the failure.

  • No duplicates

    At most one reminder per kind, loan, day, and channel.

  • Live alerts

    An authenticated WebSocket refreshes the inbox as notices arrive.

  • Scheduled or manual

    Daily Celery beat runs, or an on-demand management command.

Student portal

The library in a student's pocket

  • School ID QR

    A labelled code students present at the desk, plus copy QR codes.

  • Live dashboard

    Borrowed, due soon, overdue, and unread counts at a glance.

  • Subscriptions

    Dates, reason, copy code, and status for every loan.

  • My requests

    Decision history, notes, and cancellation for pending requests.

  • Profile and settings

    Contact details, consent state, and reminder preferences.

  • Installable app

    Add to home screen, with an offline fallback when the network drops.

Staff and administration

Running the desk with the right access

  • Console overview

    Pending, active, due today, overdue, and unreturned counts.

  • Desk queues

    Requests, borrowed books, returns, fees, and unreturned lists.

  • Filters and search

    Borrowed and fees filter by grade and section; fees search by name or School ID.

  • Student accounts

    Staff create and edit patron records and reset passwords.

  • Staff accounts

    Admins assign roles, add cellular numbers, and deactivate accounts.

  • Role-based access

    Server-side checks keep each student to their own records.

  • Sign-in and recovery

    School ID or username sign-in, with password reset by email.

  • Preserved history

    Deactivation blocks sign-in but keeps every loan and fee record.

  • Notification bell

    New requests and failed reminder deliveries surface to staff.
